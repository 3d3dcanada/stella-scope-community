Stella Scope 0.6.3 
================================

Linux (Debian/Ubuntu .deb), Windows 10 and 11 (installer) and Android (APK), all version 0.6.3.
Check each file against SHA256SUMS on the download page.

What is new
-----------
- Much smaller downloads. No 3D printer model is inside the installers any more. When you add a printer
  (or try the demo farm), its tile has a "Get the 3D model" button with the size on it: press it and only
  that printer's model is downloaded, checked against its published SHA-256, and kept for offline use.
  Nothing downloads by itself.
- The dials on a printer's page: temperatures in one column on the left, speed, flow and every fan the
  printer reports (part, hotend, electronics bay) in one column on the right, all the same size and never
  over the machine. They fit any window size, from a full-screen 4K monitor to a half-screen window to a
  phone.
- The fan wheel lists every fan the printer reports, not only the part and hotend fans.
- Spools show a small spool in their own colour; press it to pick the colour from the common filament colours or
  any colour, so you can see at a glance what is loaded.

What changed about the licence
------------------------------
- This source is open source under the GNU Affero General Public License 3.0, with no printer limit and no
  licence key.
- Safety: a heater left on with no print running or paused is turned off after 30 minutes (both nozzle and
  bed). Change the time on a printer's Heat page: 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes. An hour is the longest.

What is not supported
---------------------
- Bambu Lab and OctoPrint printers.
- Multi-material units (MMU, ERCF / Enraged Rabbit Carrot Feeder, Happy Hare) and tool-changer index control are
  planned for a future update.
- iPhone: there is no iPhone app yet. Stella Scope answers only on its own computer and through the Android app.
- The failure watch (spaghetti detection from the camera): coming soon, not part of this version.
- macOS: coming soon.
- The Windows installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your
  PC". Press More info, then Run anyway, after checking the file against its SHA-256.
- The Windows installer of 0.6.3 was built and its contents checked, but it was not run on a Windows
  computer before release. If anything is off on Windows, tell us with More, Feedback.

Your data
---------
Recordings, timelapses, reports and settings stay on your computer. Stella Scope sends nothing to 3D3D
except a feedback message you write and press Send on. It downloads only the 3D models you ask for.

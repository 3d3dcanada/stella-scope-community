# Code of conduct

Stella Scope is a project for people who build and run 3D printers, and people at every level of experience take part in it. We want it to be a place where a beginner can ask a basic question and an expert can disagree with a design without anyone being made to feel small.

## What we expect

- **Be kind and assume good faith.** Most problems are misunderstandings.
- **Be specific and constructive.** Say what is wrong and, where you can, what would fix it.
- **Critique ideas and code, never people.**
- **Respect each other's time**, including the maintainers'. This is a small team.
- **Welcome newcomers.** Everyone started somewhere.
- **Keep it on topic** and keep private information (names, addresses, keys) private.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or demeaning comments about anyone's identity or background.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Spam, advertising and unrelated promotion.
- Pressuring maintainers or contributors for fast or free work.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock issues and pull requests, and block people who break these rules, without notice for serious cases. To report a problem, email **info@3d3d.ca**. Reports are handled privately and with care.

This applies in the issue tracker, pull requests, and anywhere else people speak for or about the project.
